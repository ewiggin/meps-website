/ WHY

Many stores, many gateways, no single view of what you sell.

A company with several businesses — each with its own web shops, locations and brands — usually ends up with one payment integration per store. Each has its own credentials, webhooks and quirks: Stripe here, PayPal there, the bank's card terminal and Bizum somewhere else. Every new location means asking for another merchant account.

Sales end up scattered across half a dozen provider dashboards, and nobody can see at a glance what the whole group is selling.

Bridge Dollar sits in the middle. Stores call it instead of the provider; it handles the checkout, confirms the payment and records every sale in one place.

/ HOW A PAYMENT FLOWS

Store → Bridge Dollar → provider → Bridge Dollar → store.

The store never talks to the provider. Bridge Dollar creates the checkout, verifies the result with the provider itself and tells the store exactly once how it ended.

  1. 01

    The store asks for a checkout with its API key. Bridge Dollar records a pending sale and returns the checkout URL.

  2. 02

    The customer pays on the provider's own page — Stripe, PayPal, Mercado Pago or Redsys/Bizum.

  3. 03

    The redirect is never trusted. Bridge Dollar checks the payment directly with the provider before marking the sale.

  4. 04

    The provider's signed webhook is verified too. Whichever arrives first settles the sale.

  5. 05

    The store gets exactly one signed notification: sale.completed or sale.failed.

  6. 06

    The customer lands back on the store's success or cancel page. A background sweeper closes abandoned checkouts after 25 hours, so nothing stays pending forever.

/ TEST A GATEWAY

Know it works before a customer finds out it doesn't.

Check connection validates credentials, webhook configuration and the public URL against each provider, without charging anything.

Test payment runs a real end-to-end checkout in the provider's sandbox and follows it live: status, history and webhook deliveries. Test sales are flagged and never mixed with real ones.

/ DETAILS

Signed webhooks

Stores are notified with HMAC-signed webhooks, same scheme as Stripe, with a delivery log per sale: status code or error of every attempt.

Audit trail

Every change to a sale records who made it — dashboard, store, customer, system or provider — and what changed, shown as a timeline.

Nothing is deleted

Sales are never deleted. Stores, businesses and gateways with sales are archived: credentials revoked, history kept.
