WHO WE ARE

MEPS ("we", "us") operates this website at meps.software. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at hello@meps.software.

WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

When you fill in the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, job or role, and email address.

WHY WE COLLECT IT

We use this data for a single purpose: to get in touch with you about the message or request you submitted. We do not use it for marketing, profiling, or any other purpose.

NO THIRD-PARTY SHARING

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. It is only accessed by MEPS in order to respond to you.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

We keep your data only for as long as needed to respond to your request. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@meps.software.
